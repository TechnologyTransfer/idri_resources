Paper Submission

Submissions must be original, unpublished research papers not currently under review elsewhere. All manuscripts must be submitted in English and formatted according to the official ACET conference proceedings template. Papers must be 8 pages in length, including all figures, tables, references, and appendices. Authors of accepted papers must incorporate reviewers' feedback and submit the final, camera-ready version formatted to publication guidelines.

All research papers must be submitted electronically through the Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit (CMT). ACET is committed to fostering accessible knowledge sharing within the ASEAN digital research community. All presenters and attendees must complete their registration by defined registration deadline to confirm participation. For technical issues regarding the submission or general conference inquiries, please contact the organizing committee at acet@cadt.edu.kh.

The Microsoft CMT service was used for managing the peer-reviewing process for this conference. This service was provided for free by Microsoft and they bore all expenses, including costs for Azure cloud services as well as for software development and support.
